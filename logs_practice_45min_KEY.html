Part D — Solve for x (10–14)

10. \(x = 7\) \(2(x-4)^3 = 54 \Rightarrow (x-4)^3 = 27 \Rightarrow x-4 = 3\)
11. \(x = 6\) (exact); \(\approx 6.000\) \(5 \cdot 2^x = 320 \Rightarrow 2^x = 64 \Rightarrow x = 6\). Or by logs: \(x = \log_2(64) = 6\).
12. \(x = \log_3(4) = \dfrac{\log 4}{\log 3}\); \(\approx 1.262\) \(4 \cdot 3^x = 16 \Rightarrow 3^x = 4 \Rightarrow x \log 3 = \log 4\)
13. \(x = 29\) \(\log_2(x+3) = 5 \Rightarrow x+3 = 2^5 = 32 \Rightarrow x = 29\)
14. \(x = 5 + \dfrac{\log(28/3)}{\log 4} = 5 + \log_4\!\left(\tfrac{28}{3}\right)\); \(\approx 6.611\) \(3 \cdot 4^{x-5} = 28 \Rightarrow 4^{x-5} = \tfrac{28}{3} \Rightarrow (x-5)\log 4 = \log(28/3) \Rightarrow x-5 \approx 1.6107 \Rightarrow x \approx 6.611\)

Part E — Applications (15–16)

15a. \(B(t) = 250 \cdot 3^{t/4}\) Initial 250, triples every 4 hours.
15b. \(t \approx 15.955\) hours \(250 \cdot 3^{t/4} = 20000 \Rightarrow 3^{t/4} = 80 \Rightarrow \tfrac{t}{4} \log 3 = \log 80 \Rightarrow t = 4 \cdot \dfrac{\log 80}{\log 3}\)
16a. \(B(t) = 400 \cdot 2^{t/3}\) Initial 400, doubles every 3 hours.
16b. \(t \approx 17.897\) hours \(400 \cdot 2^{t/3} = 25000 \Rightarrow 2^{t/3} = 62.5 \Rightarrow \tfrac{t}{3} \log 2 = \log 62.5 \Rightarrow t = 3 \cdot \dfrac{\log 62.5}{\log 2}\)
