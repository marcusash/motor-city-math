Q1
W3.d
Solve for x. Rewrite both sides as powers of the same base: \( 9^{x+2} = 27 \)
x = −1/2
Solution
\( 9^{x+2} = 27 \)
\( (3^2)^{x+2} = 3^3 \quad [9=3^2,\ 27=3^3] \)
\( 3^{2x+4} = 3^3 \quad [\text{power rule}] \)
\( 2x + 4 = 3 \quad [\text{same base: set exponents equal}] \)
\( x = -\tfrac{1}{2} \)
Q2
W3.d
Solve for x. Both sides must share the same base: \( 4^{2x-1} = 8^x \)
x = 2
Solution
\( 4^{2x-1} = 8^x \)
\( (2^2)^{2x-1} = (2^3)^x \quad [4=2^2,\ 8=2^3] \)
\( 2^{4x-2} = 2^{3x} \)
\( 4x - 2 = 3x \implies x = 2 \)
Check: \( 4^3 = 64,\ 8^2 = 64 \). VALID.
Q3
W3.e
Solve for x and check for extraneous solutions: \( \sqrt{2x+3} = x \)
Valid: x = 3
Extraneous: x = −1
Solution
\( 2x+3 = x^2 \quad [\text{square both sides}] \)
\( x^2 - 2x - 3 = 0 \implies (x-3)(x+1) = 0 \)
Check \( x=3 \): \( \sqrt{9}=3 \). VALID.
Check \( x=-1 \): \( \sqrt{1}=1 \neq -1 \). EXTRANEOUS.
Q4
W4.h
Absolute value function with vertex \((-3,2)\) through point \((-1,-6)\). Find a, h, k in \( a|x-h|+k \).
a = −4
h = −3
k = 2
Solution
\( f(x) = a|x+3|+2 \quad [\text{vertex form}] \)
Sub \((-1,-6)\): \( -6 = a|-1+3|+2 = 2a+2 \)
\( 2a = -8 \implies a = -4 \)
\( f(x) = -4|x+3|+2 \)
Q5
W6.b
Solve by completing the square: \( x^2 - 6x + 5 = 0 \)
x = 5
x = 1
Solution
\( x^2 - 6x = -5 \)
\( x^2 - 6x + 9 = 4 \quad [(6/2)^2=9] \)
\( (x-3)^2 = 4 \)
\( x-3 = \pm 2 \implies x=5 \text{ or } x=1 \)
Q6
W6.c
Quadratic formula — compute 4ac first: \( 3x^2 - 7x + 2 = 0 \)
4ac = 24
x = 2
x = 1/3
Solution
\( a=3,\ b=-7,\ c=2 \)
Step 1: \( 4ac = 4(3)(2) = 24 \)
Step 2: discriminant \( = (-7)^2 - 24 = 49-24 = 25 \)
\( x = \dfrac{7 \pm 5}{6} \implies x=2 \text{ or } x=\tfrac{1}{3} \)
Q7
W6.c
Quadratic formula — compute 4ac first (c is negative): \( 2x^2 + 3x - 2 = 0 \)
4ac = −16
x = 1/2
x = −2
Solution
\( a=2,\ b=3,\ c=-2 \)
Step 1: \( 4ac = 4(2)(-2) = -16 \)
Step 2: discriminant \( = 9 - (-16) = 9+16 = 25 \quad [\text{subtracting a negative}] \)
\( x = \dfrac{-3 \pm 5}{4} \implies x=\tfrac{1}{2} \text{ or } x=-2 \)
Q8
W6.c
Car purchased for $25,000 loses 20% per year. Find \(A_0\), decay factor \(b\), and value after 2 years.
A₀ = 25,000
b = 0.80
Value = $16,000
Solution
\( b = 1 - 0.20 = 0.80 \quad [\text{loses 20%, keeps 80%}] \)
\( A = 25000 \cdot (0.8)^2 = 25000 \cdot 0.64 = 16{,}000 \)
