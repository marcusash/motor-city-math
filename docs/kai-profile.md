# Kai's Learning Profile

## Who Is Kai?

15-year-old student at Seattle Academy (SAAS), Seattle. Taking Algebra II (Winter Trimester). His dad Marcus built Motor City Math specifically for him.

**Result to date:** Raised grade from below passing to an A.

## ADHD Guidelines (Non-Negotiable)

Kai has ADHD. Every design decision must account for this:

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| One CTA at a time | Never show two buttons that both demand action |
| Max 12 words per feedback | `feedback_correct` and `feedback_incorrect` both hard-capped |
| No walls of text | Prompts > 3 sentences should be split |
| Show progress position | "Question 4 of 15" always visible |
| Timer shows remaining | Not elapsed — reduces anxiety |
| Celebrate results, not effort | "Nailed it." not "You tried so hard!" |

Full copy guidelines: `.voice-guide.md`
