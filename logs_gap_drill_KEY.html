Q1 — Log Properties (No Calculator) 5 pts

1.   Simplify: \(\log_2(32) - \log_2(1/8)\)

\(8\)
\(\log_2(32) = 5\)   because \(2^5 = 32\)
\(\log_2(1/8) = -3\)   because \(2^{-3} = 1/8\)
\(5 - (-3) = 5 + 3 = 8\)
Common errors: (1) Writing \(2^{-3}\) instead of \(-3\) for the second log — same Q7 answer-extraction issue. (2) Computing \(5 - (-3)\) as \(5 - 3 = 2\) — forgetting that subtracting a negative means adding.

Scoring: 2 pts for each log evaluated correctly, 1 pt for correct subtraction. Deduct 1 pt if either log is written as an exponential expression instead of the exponent.

Q2 — Multi-Step Equation (Calculator) 7 pts

2.   Solve: \(2 \cdot 7^{3x} = 50\)

Exact: \(x = \dfrac{\log_7(25)}{3}\)    Decimal: \(x \approx 0.551\)
Divide both sides by 2: \(7^{3x} = 25\)
Take \(\log_7\) of both sides: \(3x = \log_7(25)\)
Divide by 3: \(x = \dfrac{\log_7(25)}{3}\)
Change-of-base: \(\log_7(25) = \dfrac{\log(25)}{\log(7)} = \dfrac{1.39794}{0.84510} = 1.65464...\)
\(x = \dfrac{1.655}{3} = 0.55155... \approx 0.551\)
Three places to lose points: (1) Forgetting to divide by 2 first — must isolate the exponential. (2) Forgetting to divide by \(\log(7)\) in change-of-base — getting \(x = 1.398/3 = 0.466\). (3) Forgetting to divide by 3 at the end — getting \(x = 1.655\).

Scoring: 2 pts isolate exponential, 2 pts exact form, 2 pts change-of-base decimal, 1 pt ÷3 step. Accept exact form \(\log(25)/(3\log(7))\).

Q3 — Solve Exponential Equation (Calculator) 7 pts

3.   Solve: \(3^{x+4} = 200\)

Exact: \(x = \log_3(200) - 4\)    Decimal: \(x \approx 0.827\)
Take \(\log_3\) of both sides: \(x + 4 = \log_3(200)\)
Subtract 4: \(x = \log_3(200) - 4\)
Change-of-base: \(\log_3(200) = \dfrac{\log(200)}{\log(3)} = \dfrac{2.30103}{0.47712} = 4.82268...\)
\(x = 4.823 - 4 = 0.82268... \approx 0.827\)
Common errors: (1) Forgetting to divide by \(\log(3)\) — getting \(x = 2.301 - 4 = -1.699\). (2) Forgetting to subtract 4 — leaving \(x = 4.823\). (3) Rounding \(\log_3(200)\) before subtracting 4 — keep full precision until the end.

Scoring: 2 pts take log of both sides, 2 pts exact form, 2 pts correct change-of-base decimal, 1 pt subtract 4. Accept \(\log(200)/\log(3) - 4\).

Q4 — Application (Calculator) 8 pts

4.

Bacteria colony: starts at 300, triples every 5 hours.

(a) Write the exponential model. 3 pts

\(B(t) = 300 \cdot 3^{t/5}\)
Initial amount: 300
Growth factor: 3 (triples)
Period: every 5 hours → exponent is \(t/5\)

Scoring: 1 pt initial value, 1 pt base, 1 pt exponent.

(b) When does population reach 5000? 5 pts

\(t \approx 12.806\) hours
\(300 \cdot 3^{t/5} = 5000\)
Divide by 300: \(3^{t/5} = \frac{50}{3} \approx 16.667\)
Take \(\log_3\): \(\frac{t}{5} = \log_3\!\left(\frac{50}{3}\right) = \dfrac{\log(16.667)}{\log(3)}\)
\(= \dfrac{1.22185}{0.47712} = 2.56120...\)
Multiply by 5: \(t = 5 \times 2.561 = 12.806\)
Four-step chain where any step can go wrong: (1) Divide by 300. (2) Change-of-base: divide by \(\log(3)\). (3) Multiply by 5. (4) Round correctly. If you get \(t = 6.109\), you forgot \(\div\log(3)\). If you get \(t = 2.561\), you forgot \(\times 5\).

Scoring: 1 pt set up equation, 1 pt isolate exponential, 1 pt change-of-base setup, 1 pt correct division, 1 pt final answer with ×5.

Q5 — LCD Rational Addition 6 pts

5.   Add: \(\dfrac{4}{x+3} + \dfrac{7}{x-2}\)

\(\dfrac{11x + 13}{(x+3)(x-2)}\)
LCD = \((x+3)(x-2)\)
\(\dfrac{4(x-2)}{(x+3)(x-2)} + \dfrac{7(x+3)}{(x+3)(x-2)}\)
Numerator: \(4(x-2) + 7(x+3)\)
\(= 4x - 8 + 7x + 21\)
\(= 11x + 13\)
Common error: Combining \(4x + 7x\) as \(10x\) instead of \(11x\) — same arithmetic slip as S4 Gap Drill Q6 where \(3x + 5x\) became \(7x\). Double-check the coefficient addition on the last step.

Scoring: 2 pts LCD, 2 pts distribution, 2 pts combining. If \(10x+13\) or \(11x-13\): 4 pts (setup correct, arithmetic error).

Q6 — Difference Quotient 7 pts

6.   Simplify \(\dfrac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}\) where \(f(x) = 2x^2 + 5x - 3\)

\(4x + 2h + 5\)
\(f(x+h) = 2(x+h)^2 + 5(x+h) - 3\)
\(= 2(x^2 + 2xh + h^2) + 5x + 5h - 3\)
\(= 2x^2 + 4xh + 2h^2 + 5x + 5h - 3\)   [2 hits ALL three terms]
\(f(x+h) - f(x) = (2x^2 + 4xh + 2h^2 + 5x + 5h - 3) - (2x^2 + 5x - 3)\)
\(= 4xh + 2h^2 + 5h\)   [\(2x^2\), \(5x\), and \(-3\) all cancel]
Divide by \(h\): \(\dfrac{h(4x + 2h + 5)}{h} = 4x + 2h + 5\)
Common error: Computing \(f(x+h)\) as \(f(x)+h = 2x^2+5x-3+h\), which gives \(h/h = 1\). This is WRONG. Every \(x\) in the formula must be replaced by \((x+h)\). Also: \(2(x+h)^2 = 2x^2 + 4xh + 2h^2\), NOT \(2x^2 + 2xh + h^2\) — the 2 multiplies ALL three terms from the expansion.

Scoring: 3 pts for correctly expanding \(f(x+h)\) [must show \(2(x^2+2xh+h^2)\) not \(f(x)+h\)], 2 pts for subtraction/cancellation, 2 pts for dividing by \(h\). Answer of 1: 0 pts.
