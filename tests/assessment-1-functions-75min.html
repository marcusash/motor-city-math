Section A: Function Basics

18 minutes | 23 points
1 Read a function from a table 5 points

The table lists four ordered pairs:

x-2003
y5-1-14

Does the relation define a function? Use the repeated x-value to explain your answer, then give the range.

2 Catch the missing domain restriction 8 points

Let \(f(x) = \sqrt{5 - x}\) and \(g(x) = x^2 - 1\). A student says the domain of \((f/g)(x)\) is all \(x \le 5\).

Write the quotient, identify every excluded input the student missed, and give the domain in interval notation.

3 Find and compare two compositions 10 points

Let A(x) = 2x - 1 and B(x) = x2 + 3.

  1. (a) Find and simplify B(A(x)).
  2. (b) Evaluate B(A(4)).
  3. (c) Evaluate A(B(4)) and compare it with your answer to part (b).

5 Evaluate and graph a piecewise rule 8 points

Let \(p(x) = \begin{cases}2 - x & x < 1 \\ x + 2 & x \ge 1\end{cases}\).

  1. (a) Find p(0), p(1), and p(3).
  2. (b) Graph both pieces. Show the open and closed endpoint at the boundary x = 1.

Each square is 1 unit. The axes are numbered every 2 units.

6 Graph a square-root translation 10 points

Translate the parent graph \(y = \sqrt{x}\) left 4 units and down 3 units. Write its rule, then graph it. Mark its starting point and three more points. State its domain and range, and check whether \((-3, -2)\) lies on the graph.

Each square is 1 unit. The axes are numbered every 2 units.

Section C: Polynomial Structure and Graphs

20 minutes | 27 points
7 Recover a polynomial from its graph 7 points

The graph is a cubic polynomial with a repeated zero. Use its intercepts and turning points to write an equation in factored form. Then state where it is increasing and decreasing, and give its relative maximum and minimum points.

8 Choose the polynomial that fits the graph behavior 10 points

A polynomial graph crosses the x-axis at -2, touches and turns at 1, falls to the left, and rises to the right. Which rule could represent it?

  1. (a) Choose one and explain how its zeros, multiplicities, and leading term match.

    A. (x + 2)(x - 1)2

    B. -(x + 2)2(x - 1)

    C. (x + 2)2(x - 1)2

    D. -(x + 2)(x - 1)2

9 Check a classmate's graph analysis 10 points

The graph of r is made of connected line segments through the plotted vertices. A classmate reports: domain [-5, 5], range [-3, 4], zeros -4, 0, and 4; increasing on (-5, -3) and (2, 5); relative maximum y = 4 and relative minimum y = -3.

Check each part of the report and correct any errors. Give the increasing and decreasing intervals, and write the relative maximum and minimum as coordinate points.

Section D: Function Models and Transformations

15 minutes | 22 points
10 Choose a valid composition using units 12 points

A rental shop charges D(d) = 9d + 12 dollars for d days. A tax function converts a pre-tax price c dollars to T(c) = 1.08c dollars.

  1. (a) Write the composition that gives the after-tax rental cost, and explain why its order fits the units.
  2. (b) Simplify the composite rule.
  3. (c) Find the total cost for a 4-day rental.
  4. (d) Explain why reversing the order does not model this situation.
11 Keep the original domain when composing 10 points

Let \(f(x) = \sqrt{x + 1}\) and \(g(x) = x^2 - 1\).

  1. (a) Find and simplify g(f(x)). State its domain, and explain why simplifying the square root does not make the original domain larger.
  2. (b) Find and simplify f(g(x)). State its domain. Explain why its domain differs from part (a).
