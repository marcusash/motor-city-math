Section 1 — Evaluating Expressions & Function Notation 14 pts

Q1. 2 pts

Evaluate \(3x - 5\) when \(x = 4\).

\(3(4) - 5 = 12 - 5 = \) 7

Q2. 4 pts

Evaluate \(2a^2 - 3b\) when \(a = -2\) and \(b = 5\).

\(2(-2)^2 - 3(5) = 2(4) - 15 = 8 - 15 = \) −7

Q3. 4 pts

Evaluate \(\dfrac{5x - y}{2}\) when \(x = 3\) and \(y = -1\).

\(\dfrac{5(3) - (-1)}{2} = \dfrac{15 + 1}{2} = \dfrac{16}{2} = \) 8

Q4. 4 pts

Let \(f(x) = 3x - 1\).

(a) Find \(f(-2)\).

\(f(-2) = 3(-2) - 1 = -6 - 1 = \) −7

(b) Find the value of \(x\) where \(f(x) = 11\).

\(3x - 1 = 11 \;\Rightarrow\; 3x = 12 \;\Rightarrow\; x = \) 4

Section 2 — Solving Equations 30 pts

Q5. 4 pts

\(2x + 9 = 21\)

\(2x = 12 \;\Rightarrow\; x = \) 6

Q6. 4 pts

\(3(x - 4) = 15\)

\(3x - 12 = 15 \;\Rightarrow\; 3x = 27 \;\Rightarrow\; x = \) 9

Q7. 6 pts

\(4x - 7 = x + 8\)

\(4x - x = 8 + 7 \;\Rightarrow\; 3x = 15 \;\Rightarrow\; x = \) 5

Q8. 6 pts

\(2(3x + 1) = 4x - 6\)

\(6x + 2 = 4x - 6 \;\Rightarrow\; 2x = -8 \;\Rightarrow\; x = \) −4

Q9. 4 pts

\(\dfrac{2}{3}x - 4 = 6\)

\(\tfrac{2}{3}x = 10 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 10 \cdot \tfrac{3}{2} \;\Rightarrow\; x = \) 15

Q10. 3 pts

\(5x + 2 = 5x - 3\)

Subtract \(5x\): \(2 = -3\) — false. No solution.

Q11. 3 pts

\(4(x - 1) = 4x - 4\)

Distribute: \(4x - 4 = 4x - 4 \;\Rightarrow\; 0 = 0\) — always true. Infinitely many solutions.

Section 3 — Proportional Relationships 18 pts

Q12. 6 pts

Table: (0,3), (2,7), (4,11), (6,15)

(a) Not proportional — when \(x=0\), \(y=3 \ne 0\). A proportional relationship passes through the origin.
(b) y-intercept = 3
(c) Slope: \(\Delta y / \Delta x = 4/2 = 2\). Equation: \(y = 2x + 3\)

Q13. 6 pts

Line through \((0,-2)\) and \((4,6)\).

(a) Not proportional — y-intercept is \(-2\), not 0.
(b) \(m = \dfrac{6 - (-2)}{4 - 0} = \dfrac{8}{4} = \) 2
(c) \(y = 2x - 2\)

Q14. 6 pts

Parking garage: \ entry + \/hour.

(a) \(C = 3h + 5\)
(b) Slope = cost increases by \ per additional hour (the per-hour rate).
(c) Not proportional — the \ flat fee means \(C = 5\) when \(h = 0\), so the y-intercept is 5, not 0.

y-intercept: (0, -3). Slope \(2 = \tfrac{2}{1}\): from y-int, go up 2 right 1 to (1, -1), then again to (2, 1). Line continues both directions.
