Q1. Cubic Graph — \(f(x) = x^3 - 2x^2 - 5x + 6\) S1 / Poly
(a) Factor & zeros
\(f(1) = 1 - 2 - 5 + 6 = 0\) ✓  →  divide by \((x-1)\): synthetic or long division gives \((x-1)(x^2-x-6) = (x-1)(x-3)(x+2)\)
Zeros: \(x = -2, \; 1, \; 3\) (all multiplicity 1 — graph crosses at each)
(b) \(y\)-intercept & end behavior
\(y\)-intercept: \(f(0) = 6 \Rightarrow (0, 6)\)
Leading term \(+x^3\):   As \(x\to+\infty,\; f\to+\infty\)    As \(x\to-\infty,\; f\to-\infty\)
Odd degree, positive leading coefficient → bottom-left to top-right.
(c) Sketch — key features
Crosses x-axis at \(x=-2, 1, 3\).   \(y\)-intercept at \((0,6)\).
Local max between \(x=-2\) and \(x=1\); local min between \(x=1\) and \(x=3\). Starts bottom-left, ends top-right.
Q2. Quartic Graph — \(g(x) = x^4 - 2x^3 - 3x^2\) S1 / Poly
(a) Factor & zeros with multiplicity
\(g(x) = x^2(x^2-2x-3) = x^2(x-3)(x+1)\)
Zeros: \(x=0\) (mult. 2 — bounces),   \(x=3\) (mult. 1 — crosses),   \(x=-1\) (mult. 1 — crosses)
(b) End behavior & cross/bounce
Leading term \(+x^4\) (even degree, positive):   As \(x\to\pm\infty,\; g\to+\infty\) — both ends UP.
\(x=0\): even multiplicity → touches and bounces back. \(x=-1\) and \(x=3\): odd multiplicity → crosses through.
(c) Sketch features
\(y\)-intercept: \(g(0) = 0\). Graph bounces at origin, crosses at \(x=-1\) and \(x=3\), both ends go up.
Q3–Q5. Log Evaluation S5 / Logs

Answer: 6
Q4. \(\log_3\!\left(\tfrac{1}{27}\right)\)
Rewrite: \(3^? = \tfrac{1}{27} = 3^{-3}\)
Answer: −3

Part 2 — Calculator Allowed · 30 points
Q6–Q9. Factoring S1 / Poly
Q6. \(x^2 - 9\)
\((x+3)(x-3)\)
Difference of squares: \(a^2 - b^2 = (a+b)(a-b)\)
Q7. \(3x^3 - 12x\)
\(3x(x+2)(x-2)\)
GCF = \(3x\): \(3x(x^2-4)\), then difference of squares.
Q8. \(x^2 - 7x + 12\)
\((x-3)(x-4)\)
Need two numbers that multiply to 12 and add to −7: \(-3\) and \(-4\).
Q9. \(x^3 + 8\)
\((x+2)(x^2-2x+4)\)
Sum of cubes: \(a^3+b^3=(a+b)(a^2-ab+b^2)\); here \(a=x, b=2\).
Q10–Q13. Polynomial Division S1 / PLD
Q10. \((x^3 - 3x^2 + 5x - 3) \div (x-1)\)
Synthetic with \(x=1\):   1 | 1   −3   5   −3  →  1   −2   3   0
Answer: \(x^2 - 2x + 3\)   (R = 0)
Q11. \((2x^3 + x^2 - 7x + 4) \div (x-1)\)
Synthetic with \(x=1\):   1 | 2   1   −7   4  →  2   3   −4   0
Answer: \(2x^2 + 3x - 4\)   (R = 0; further factors as \((2x-1)(x+4)\))
Q12. \((x^3 + 2x^2 - 4x - 8) \div (x+2)\)
Synthetic with \(x=-2\):   −2 | 1   2   −4   −8  →  1   0   −4   0
Answer: \(x^2 - 4 = (x+2)(x-2)\)   (R = 0)
Q13. \((x^3 + 4x^2 - 2x + 3) \div (x+2)\)   (remainder expected)
Synthetic with \(x=-2\):   −2 | 1   4   −2   3  →  1   2   −6   15
Answer: \(x^2 + 2x - 6 + \dfrac{15}{x+2}\)

Q18. Linear Model S6 / Modeling

Q19. Quadratic Model — \(h(t) = -16t^2 + 40t + 6\) S6 / Modeling
(a) Maximum height
\(t = -\dfrac{40}{2(-16)} = \dfrac{40}{32} = 1.25\) sec
\(h(1.25) = -16(1.5625) + 40(1.25) + 6 = -25 + 50 + 6\)
Max height = 31 ft at \(t = 1.25\) sec
(b) Hits ground (\(h = 0\))
\(-16t^2+40t+6=0 \Rightarrow 8t^2-20t-3=0\)
Quadratic formula: \(t = \dfrac{20 \pm \sqrt{400+96}}{16} = \dfrac{20 \pm \sqrt{496}}{16}\)
\(t \approx 2.64\) sec   (reject negative root)
(c) Height = 30 ft
\(-16t^2+40t+6=30 \Rightarrow -16t^2+40t-24=0 \Rightarrow 2t^2-5t+3=0 \Rightarrow (2t-3)(t-1)=0\)
\(t = 1\) sec and \(t = 1.5\) sec
Q20. Exponential Model S6 / Modeling — Gap standard: stress growth-rate step
(a) Find base \(b\)   [S6 gap — Kai struggled with isolating the rate]
\(11500 = 8000 \cdot b^{10} \Rightarrow b^{10} = \dfrac{11500}{8000} = 1.4375\)
\(b = 1.4375^{1/10} = 1.4375^{0.1}\)
\(b \approx 1.0369\)   (growth rate ≈ 3.69%/yr)
Key step: divide first, then take the 10th root. Common error: students try to log both sides before isolating \(b^{10}\).
(b) Population in 2030 (\(t = 20\))
\(P(20) = 8000 \cdot (1.0369)^{20} \approx 8000 \cdot 2.069\)
\(\approx 16{,}552\)
(c) When does population double to 16,000?
\(16000 = 8000 \cdot (1.0369)^t \Rightarrow 2 = (1.0369)^t\)
\(t = \dfrac{\log 2}{\log 1.0369} = \dfrac{0.30103}{0.01573}\)
\(t \approx 19.13\) years  →  Year \(\approx 2029\)
Change-of-base required. Confirm student used their own \(b\) value — slight variation in \(t\) is acceptable.
