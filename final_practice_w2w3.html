W2.c / W2.e — Graphing (5–6)

5. Parent: \(|x|\). Stretch ×2, left 3, down 1. Domain: all reals. Range: \(y \ge -1\). Vertex: (\(-3, -1\)). x-intercepts: \(2|x+3|=1 \Rightarrow |x+3|=\tfrac{1}{2} \Rightarrow x=-2.5\) or \(x=-3.5\)
6. Parent: \(x^2\). Reflect, right 1, up 4. Domain: all reals. Range: \(y \le 4\). Vertex: (1, 4). x-intercepts: \(-(x-1)^2+4=0 \Rightarrow (x-1)^2=4 \Rightarrow x=3\) or \(x=-1\) f(x)=3: \((x-1)^2=1 \Rightarrow x=2\) or \(x=0\) → points (2,3) and (0,3)

W3 — Solving (7–12)

7. \(x = \tfrac{7}{3}\) \(2^{3x-1}=2^6 \Rightarrow 3x-1=6 \Rightarrow 3x=7\)
8. \(x = 0\) or \(x = -3\) \(3(2x+3)^2=27 \Rightarrow (2x+3)^2=9 \Rightarrow 2x+3=\pm3\)
9. \(x = -\tfrac{1}{4}\) \(5=4(3x+2)=12x+8 \Rightarrow 12x=-3\)
10. \(x = \pm\sqrt{13}\) \(\sqrt{3+x^2}=4 \Rightarrow 3+x^2=16 \Rightarrow x^2=13\)
11. \(x = 0\) \(2(x-2)^3=-16 \Rightarrow (x-2)^3=-8 \Rightarrow x-2=-2\)
12. \(x = \pm 2\) \(4^{x^2-1}=64=4^3 \Rightarrow x^2-1=3 \Rightarrow x^2=4\)

W3 — More Solving (17–22)

17. \(x = \tfrac{1}{4}\) \(4^{2-\sqrt{x}}=2^{2(2-\sqrt{x})}=2^3 \Rightarrow 4-2\sqrt{x}=3 \Rightarrow \sqrt{x}=\tfrac{1}{2} \Rightarrow x=\tfrac{1}{4}\) Check: \(4^{2-1/2}=4^{3/2}=8\) ✓
18. \(x = -2\) \(5-2x=9 \Rightarrow -2x=4\). Check: \(\sqrt{5+4}=\sqrt{9}=3\) ✓
19. \(x = \tfrac{13}{2}\) \(4x+1=6(x-2)=6x-12 \Rightarrow -2x=-13\). Check: \(\tfrac{26+1}{6.5-2}=\tfrac{27}{4.5}=6\) ✓
20. \(x = -\tfrac{19}{9}\) \(\tfrac{3}{x+2}=-27 \Rightarrow 3=-27x-54 \Rightarrow 27x=-57\). Check: ✓ (no extraneous)
21. \(x = 4\) \(3x-2=x+6 \Rightarrow 2x=8\). Check: \(\sqrt{10}=\sqrt{10}\) ✓ (no extraneous)
22. ★ \(x = \pm125\) \(2x^{2/3}=50 \Rightarrow x^{2/3}=25 \Rightarrow x=25^{3/2}=(\sqrt{25})^3=5^3=125\) Also \(x=-125\): \((-125)^{2/3}=(\sqrt[3]{-125})^2=(-5)^2=25\) ✓ CUBE CHECK: 5³=125. Know your cubes!
