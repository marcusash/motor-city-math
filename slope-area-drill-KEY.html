Problem 1 — Slope on a Mountain Road
Core idea
Percent grade = rise ÷ run × 100. Once you have the grade as a decimal, the relationship is linear: \(V = m \cdot H\) where \(m\) is the grade decimal. Everything else follows from this one equation.
(a) Percent grade
\(\text{grade} = \dfrac{42}{700} = 0.06 = 6\%\)
6%
(b) Linear equation
Grade decimal = 0.06, so for every foot of horizontal distance, drop = 0.06 ft
\(V = 0.06H\)
\(V = 0.06\,H\)
(c) Horizontal distance when V = 84 ft
Substitute V = 84 into equation
\(84 = 0.06\,H\)
Solve for H
\(H = \dfrac{84}{0.06} = 1{,}400 \text{ ft}\)
1,400 feet
⚠ Arithmetic check
\(0.06 \times 1400 = 84\) ✓ — always verify by plugging back in.
(d) Total height when H = 2,500 ft
\(V = 0.06 \times 2500 = 150 \text{ ft}\)
150 feet
Problem 2 — Area Bounded by a Line
Strategy
Identify what shape each region is. Read off the heights by evaluating \(f(x)\) at the boundary x-values. Apply the correct area formula. The verification step in (e) is a built-in check — if your numbers don't add up, one of your areas is wrong.
(a) x-intercept of f(x) = −2x + 10
Set f(x) = 0
\(-2x + 10 = 0\)
\(2x = 10 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 5\)
x-intercept: \((5,\, 0)\)
(b) Heights at x = 0 and x = 3
\(f(0) = -2(0) + 10 = 10\)
\(f(3) = -2(3) + 10 = 4\)
\(f(0) = 10\)    \(f(3) = 4\)
(c) Left region — trapezoid [x = 0 to x = 3]
Identify the shape
Parallel sides: \(b_1 = f(0) = 10\) and \(b_2 = f(3) = 4\). Width (height of trapezoid): \(h = 3\).
Apply formula
\(A = \tfrac{1}{2}(b_1 + b_2) \times h = \tfrac{1}{2}(10 + 4)(3) = \tfrac{1}{2}(14)(3) = 21\)
Left area = 21 sq units
(d) Right region — triangle [x = 3 to x = 5]
Identify the shape
Base: from \(x = 3\) to \(x = 5\), so base \(= 2\). Height: \(f(3) = 4\).
Apply formula
\(A = \tfrac{1}{2} \times 2 \times 4 = 4\)
Right area = 4 sq units
⚠ Common error
The base is \(5 - 3 = 2\), not 5. The triangle goes from x = 3 to the x-intercept, not from x = 0.
(e) Total area — verify
Whole triangle: base = 5 (x-intercept), height = f(0) = 10
\(A = \tfrac{1}{2} \times 5 \times 10 = 25\)
Total area = 25 sq units
✓   21 + 4 = 25   — checks out
