Q11 other difference quotient function substitution 0 / 1
Simplify the difference quotient \(\dfrac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}\) where \(f(x)=3x^2-2x+1\).
Kai's Answer
\(1\)
Correct Answer
\(6x+3h-2\)
Didn't expand f(x+h) — treated it as f(x)+h
Kai computed \(f(x+h)\) as \(f(x)+h = 3x^2-2x+1+h\), then subtracted \(f(x)\) to get \(h\), divided by \(h\) to get \(1\). But \(f(x+h)\) means substitute \((x+h)\) for every \(x\): \(f(x+h)=3(x+h)^2-2(x+h)+1\). Expand: \(3(x^2+2xh+h^2)-2x-2h+1 = 3x^2+6xh+3h^2-2x-2h+1\). Subtract \(f(x)\): \(6xh+3h^2-2h\). Factor out \(h\): \(h(6x+3h-2)\). Divide by \(h\): \(6x+3h-2\).
Coaching Note
Same concept as Assessment 2A Q14. \(f(x+h) \neq f(x)+h\). Every \(x\) in the formula gets replaced by \((x+h)\).
