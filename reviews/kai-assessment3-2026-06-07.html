Q2 S5 log domain ✓ Full credit
Explain why \(\log_3(-4)\) is undefined.
Kai’s Explanation
\(\log_3(-4) = x\) would require \(3^x = -4\). A positive base raised to any power is always positive, so \(3^x \gt 0\) for all \(x\). It cannot equal \(-4\). Therefore the expression is undefined.
