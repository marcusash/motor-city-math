# Direct student print-usability review

The current executor simulated an A student's use of the approved revision 2
and inspected all nine student PDF pages and all eight worked-key PDF pages.
This is a same-session perspective, not an independent model review or measured
pen-and-paper attempt.

Response-space checks:

- Q1: seven correction cells plus direction reasoning on page 1; a full
  symmetric -8 to 8 graph and separate reasoning/calculation lines on page 2.
- Q2: five lines for the two models/domains and eight for percentages, target
  conclusions and checks. No model equations are supplied in the student copy.
- Q3: six lines for the inverse and exchanged sets, six for two symbolic checks.
- Q4: nine half-width lines for both inverse rules/sets and comparison, a shared
  graph for reflection, and three full-width reasoning lines. Required boundary
  points (-1,6) and (6,-1) fit the labeled window.
- Q5: eight half-width lines for evaluations/sets, a graph, and five full-width
  lines for candidate and boundary checks.
- Q6: the actual source polyline is supplied and includes both endpoints;
  eight full-width response lines follow.
- Q7: nine half-width reasoning lines, three full-width calculation lines and
  a graph labeled at four y-units per square. The required p(2)=16 point fits.
- Q8: nine half-width analysis lines, four full-width division/end-behavior
  lines and a graph labeled at two y-units per square. Visible portions of both
  branches fit the vertical -12 to 12 window.

Formatting repairs: restored explicit question numbers, widened Q1 correction
cells, enlarged its parent-function fraction, and reduced only Q1's worked-key
graph slightly to avoid the footer. The approved givens, requirements, answers
and scoring did not change. Changed-page PDF images were inspected again.

Large displayed fractions, powers, roots and interval symbols are readable.
Source and answer graphs have symmetric windows, labeled scales and enough
room for the requested features. Writing-space judgments use approximate
handwritten line counts and mentally placed coordinates, not actual handwriting.

Existing exporter checks passed: nine student pages, eight key pages, loaded
math fonts, no math errors and no detected content/footer overlap. No broad
site test suite or new validation harness was run for this standalone packet.
