## 1. Rational claim correction - 16 points

$$
F(x)=1+\frac{4}{x-1}.
$$

Domain and range:

$$
(-\infty,1)\cup(1,\infty).

$$

## 2. Fundraiser expense models - 14 points

Total donations before expenses are 500+x under either plan.
Plan A expenses are 100+0.10x; plan B expenses stay at 100.

$$
E_A(x)=\frac{100+0.10x}{500+x},\qquad x\ge0.
$$

$$
E_B(x)=\frac{100}{500+x},\qquad x\ge0.
$$

Both domains:

$$
[0,\infty).
$$

x is measured in dollars.
Initially, 100/500=20%. At x=500:
plan A has (100+50)/1000=15%;
plan B has 100/1000=10%.

For an exact 10% expense percentage under A:

$$
100+0.10x=0.10(500+x)=50+0.10x.
$$

This would require 100=50, so no additional amount works.
Equivalently:

$$
E_A(x)=0.10+\frac{50}{500+x}>0.10 \quad \text{for } x\ge0.
$$

The expense fraction approaches 10% but never reaches it.
Under B, 100=0.10(500+x), so x=500 dollars.
Check: 100 dollars in expenses / 1000 dollars collected =10%.

Credit: both models 4; meaningful domains 2; initial/later percentages 3;
plan A impossibility with justification 3; plan B amount and totals check 2.

## 8. Hole plus derived slant - 14 points

$$

$$

$$
