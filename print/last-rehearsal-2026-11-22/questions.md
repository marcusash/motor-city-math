# Approved final rehearsal

75-minute target; 90-minute maximum; 100 points.

## 1. Cubic graph and analysis (7 points)

Graph the function using at least five points:

$$
f(x)=2(-x+2)^3-2.
$$

Describe both end behaviors.

Describe its transformations from:

$$
y=x^3.
$$

## 2. Derive a square-root function (8 points)

A transformation of the square-root parent function has starting point (-4,2)
and passes through (0,1). There are no additional domain restrictions.

1. Find its equation.
2. Describe its transformations from the square-root parent function.
3. Graph it using the starting point and at least two more points. Label each
   coordinate pair.
4. State its domain and range in interval notation.
5. Find its x- and y-intercepts, if they exist.

## 3. Polynomial structure (3 points)

Use:

$$
f(x)=2x^5-3x^2+x-4.
$$

1. State the degree and leading coefficient, then describe both end behaviors.
2. What is the greatest possible number of turning points?
3. What is the greatest possible number of x-intercepts?

No graph required.

## 4. Division, factoring and graph shape (10 points)

Use:

$$
p(x)=x^3+2x^2-5x-6.
$$

You are told that x=2 is a zero.

1. Factor p(x) completely. (Hint: consider using polynomial division.)
2. Find all x-intercepts and state whether the graph crosses or touches the
   axis at each.
3. Describe both end behaviors.
4. Calculate p(-2) and p(1).
5. Sketch the graph, labeling its intercepts and the two calculated points.

Exact turning points are not required.

## 5. Operations with a rational function (7 points)

Let:

$$
f(x)=\frac{x+1}{x-2},\qquad g(x)=2x-1.
$$

a) Find and simplify f(g(x)). State any excluded input.

b) Find and simplify g(f(x)). State any excluded input. Are the two functions
the same? Explain.

c) Solve f(g(x))=0, and check your answer.

No graph required.

## 6. Read functions from a table and a graph (5 points)

Function f is defined only at the inputs in this table.

| x | -2 | -1 | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| f(x) | 3 | 1 | -2 | 0 | 2 |

Use the supplied graph of g.

a) Find (2f-g)(0).

b) Find g(f(-2)).

c) Find (f/g)(0). If undefined, explain why.

d) Find f(g(-3)). If undefined, explain why.

## 7. Three-piece function (10 points)

$$
f(x)=\begin{cases}
-x-1, & x<-2\\
x^2-2, & -2\le x<2\\
x+1, & x\ge2
\end{cases}
$$

a) Graph f. Show open and closed circles correctly at both boundaries.

b) Find f(-2), f(0), and f(2).

c) State the domain and range in interval notation.

d) Solve f(x)=2. Show the candidate solutions from each piece, check each
against that piece's condition, and give the complete solution set.

## 8. Water-tank model (5 points)

A tank starts with 20 liters of water. During the first 4 minutes, water is
added at 5 liters per minute. From minute 4 to minute 8, no water is added or
removed. From minute 8 to minute 12, water drains at 10 liters per minute.
Consider only the time interval from 0 through 12 minutes, including both ends.

Let V(t) be the amount of water in the tank, in liters, t minutes after the
process starts.

a) Write a piecewise function for V(t), including each piece's time interval.

b) At what times does the tank contain exactly 30 liters? Show your work and
check each time against the relevant piece's interval.

No graph is required.

## 10. Free-throw accuracy (4 points)

During basketball practice, a player has made 12 of 20 free-throw attempts.
He then makes the next x free throws without missing. The fraction of all his
attempts that he has made is

$$A(x)=\frac{12+x}{20+x}.$$

a) Find A(0) and explain what it means.

b) Find A(20) and write it as a percent.

No graph is required.

## 11. Build and graph a rational function (8 points)

a) Write one rational function with all these features:

- A vertical asymptote at x=-2.
- A hole at x=-1.
- An x-intercept at x=-4.
- A horizontal asymptote at y=1.

b) Find the coordinates of the hole and the y-intercept. Explain why the
hole is not a vertical asymptote.

c) Use these features to sketch your function. Show both asymptotes,
the hole, and both intercepts clearly.

## 12. Asymptote types and a slant-function graph (10 points)

a) For each function below, decide whether it has a horizontal asymptote,
a slant asymptote, or neither. Find the asymptote if there is one.

$$A(x)=\frac{3x^2+1}{x^2+2},\qquad
B(x)=\frac{x-2}{x^2+1},\qquad
C(x)=\frac{x^3+1}{x+2}.$$

For parts b-e, use

$$f(x)=\frac{x^2+x-6}{x-1}.$$

b) Factor the numerator and find the x- and y-intercepts.

c) Find the vertical asymptote. Use long division to find the slant asymptote.

d) Describe the end behavior using arrow notation. Also state which line
the graph approaches at both distant ends.

e) Sketch f using these features. Show both asymptotes and all intercepts.

## 13. Construct, check and graph inverses (9 points)

a) Let f(x)=sqrt(x-2)+1. State the domain and range of f. Construct
f^(-1)(x) and state its domain and range. Show your algebra.

b) The table gives points on h(x)=x^2+1.

| x | -2 | -1 | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| h(x) | 5 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 5 |

Write the inverse coordinate pair for each listed point. No graph is required
for this part.

c) Is the inverse of h a function or a relation? Explain. Give two different
interval restrictions on the domain of h that make it one-to-one, and write
the inverse function for each restriction.

d) The supplied graph of k consists of straight line segments. Draw its
inverse on the separate blank grid. Clearly show the endpoints and corners.
Name one point that lies on both k and its inverse. Explain why that point
must be on both graphs.

## 14. Use an inverse in context (4 points)

A bicycle rental costs an $8 starting fee plus $4 per hour, including
fractional hours. The total cost in dollars is C(t)=4t+8, where t>=0
is the rental time in hours.

a) Construct C^(-1)(x).

b) Find C^(-1)(28).

c) Explain what your answer to part b means in this situation. Use units.

d) Check your answer to part b by evaluating C at that value.

No graph is required.
